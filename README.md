## 📜 About

**JASS Urdu Shayari Explorer** is a desktop application for exploring a multilingual collection of Urdu poetry through a clean, searchable and completely local workspace.

The project brings together **Urdu original text**, **Hindi representation**, and **English representation** of poetry in a structured SQLite corpus.

It is designed for:

- 📖 Literary reading
- 🔎 Multilingual searching
- 🌐 Language comparison
- 📚 Corpus exploration
- 🧠 Digital humanities research
- 🗂️ Local literary archiving
- 🧪 Future language and NLP research

The goal is simple:

> **Preserve literary material faithfully, make it searchable, and create a useful environment for exploring it.**
